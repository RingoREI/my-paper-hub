# My Paper Hub

A personal knowledge base for paper notes, critical reading, and research synthesis in neuroscience and predictive processing.

## Topics

- **Interoception & Emotion** — Bodily signals, emotional experience, and mental health.
- **Predictive Processing** — Predictive coding, active inference, and theories of emotion.
- **Brain–Heart Interactions** — Neural dynamics, cardiac signals, and brain–body coupling.

## Repository Structure

```text
my-paper-hub/
├── papers/          # Individual paper notes
├── topic-reviews/   # Cross-paper synthesis and literature reviews
├── paper-club/      # Presentation notes and discussion materials
├── research-ideas/  # Open questions and emerging research ideas
├── templates/       # Reusable note templates
└── README.md
```

## How I Read Papers

My notes focus on:

- Research questions and theoretical background
- Experimental design and analytical methods
- Main findings and their interpretation
- Limitations and unresolved questions
- Connections to other work and potential research directions
- My own reflection on the work/questions about the work

## Purpose

This repository documents my ongoing learning and research thinking. It is intended to be a growing knowledge base rather than a collection of paper summaries.

## Notes

All notes represent my own understanding and interpretation of the cited work. Please consult the original publications for authoritative descriptions of the research.
